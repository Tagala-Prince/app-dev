## Characters:
- **Loki**: the god of mischief
- **Mobius**: TVA agent who partners with Loki
- **Sylvie**: another variant of Loki
- **He Who Remains**: the man who controls the timeline

## Explanation:

- Season 1: in season one there is a variant that there are hunting and in which the variant turns out to be Sylvie another version of Loki who was taken by the TVA as a child. Loki and Sylvie uncover the truth the TVA’s “Time Keepers” are fabrications, and the agency is run by He Who Remains a being who ended a multiversal war by controlling all of time wherein He offers Loki and Sylvie a choice to kill him and unleash chaos or take over his role wherein Sylvie kills him and the timeline branches into a multiverse. Loki is left stranded back at a TVA that no longer recognizes him with a statue of Kang the Conqueror looming.

- Season 2: in season two Loki finds himself “time slipping” uncontrollably where he is jumping between moments in the TVA’s past and futur. With Mobius Hunter B-15 and others he works to stabilize the Temporal Loom the machine that keeps the timeline branches from collapsing. Meanwhile Sylvie returns along with Ouroboros “O.B.” and Victor Timely. After a series of failed attempts to fix things Loki learns that the only way to save the multiverse is to sacrifice himself. He gains the power to time-slip at will, then chooses to stay in the Temporal Loom holding all the timelines together and then he becomes a god of stories “sitting on the throne at the end of time,” a fitting end to his arc from a self-serving trickster to a selfless hero.
